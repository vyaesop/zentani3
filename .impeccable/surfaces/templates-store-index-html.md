## Scope

Homepage (`templates/store/index.html`, `templates/store/_home_sections.html`). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: mixed men and women in Addis, streetwear-leaning, phone-first, Telegram/Instagram-native. Job: see what dropped, trust the store, start an order. Proof on hand: real product photos, real fees, real phone number, real channel. No testimonials exist; none may be invented.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One woven band is the page's entire structure; the products are the cloth. It refuses the photo-hero plus four-up grid every fashion store ships.

OWN-WORLD: Cotton-white ground, ink-black type (Unbounded display, Archivo body, Noto Sans Ethiopic for Amharic), four thread colours (red, gold, green, indigo) living only inside authored SVG tibeb bands. Depth by overlap, never shadow. Tabular numerals for every fee, size and time. Black selvedge buttons with a thread-coloured edge.

STORY: The shopper recognises the band as Ethiopian and modern, sees three real pieces, reads "check it with the driver, then pay" and the fee ruler, and either shops the drop or orders on Telegram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: 390px phone. Logo mark and cart in the header. A full-bleed band weaves in once, left to right. Two-line headline. Three product photos overlapping as swatches. Truth line with a gold thread underline. Fee ruler in tabular figures. Primary action Shop the drop (black bar), secondary Order on Telegram.

FORM: Tibeb Band, candidate 7 of 7, assigned by roll; seed 0a6966cd.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
