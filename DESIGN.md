# Design System: Zentanee

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Tibeb Band"**

Every storefront page is a length of white cotton with one dense woven border. The *tibeb* (the patterned band on Ethiopian cotton dress) is the only ornament and the only structure: a thin woven line closes the header on every page (the homepage swaps it for its full hero band), a full band opens the footer, and everything between is ink on cotton: black type, 2px black rules, black-framed photos, black buttons. The two thread colours, indigo and green, live inside the woven tiles with ink; indigo alone leaves the weave, and only ever as a line. Depth is physical (things overlap) and drawn (2px rules); nothing casts a shadow, nothing is rounded, nothing is tinted.

The system is the whole storefront. `zent-tibeb.css` carries tokens, type, shell and components on `body.zh-body`; `zent-tibeb-bands.css` carries the generated tiles; `zent-tibeb-pages.css` carries catalog, product, commerce and account layouts; `zent-home.css` carries the homepage hero and sections. All classes use the `.zh-` prefix (a few legacy hooks keep `zent-`: search suggestions, alerts, toast, the drawer wrapper). The Control Room dashboard (`zent-core.css`, `zent-dashboard.css`) is a separate world and is out of scope. The pop-art halftone black-and-white circular logo is the binding identity asset; the palette is chosen to sit under it, and PRODUCT.md's binding commitment (no red, no yellow anywhere on the storefront) is what removed the earlier red and gold threads.

Density is confident-sparse: a small catalogue shown as a few large modules on a ruled grid, never padded to fill; lists are rows separated by rules, never boxed cards; forms are ruled slips. Amharic swaps both type stacks to Noto Sans Ethiopic with more leading and no negative tracking; palette and rules are unchanged. Product photos in the local fixture are placeholder jewellery; the brand's imagery is supplier photography on plain backgrounds and is not described by this system.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Cotton-white ground with ink type; the two thread colours appear as SVG fills inside the woven tiles, and indigo alone appears outside them, as a 3px or 6px line.
- One woven band motif, authored as SVG geometry (120x64 full, 24x12 thin) and tiled as CSS backgrounds in three tints: default (ink lead), indigo, green.
- Depth by overlap and 2px ink rules only; no `box-shadow`, no gradient, no blur, no radius anywhere on the storefront.
- Tabular lining numerals on every price, fee, count, size, quantity, order number and time; money is set in Unbounded 600.
- Buttons are ink bars in Unbounded; the primary carries a 6px indigo bottom edge (the selvedge).
- State is inversion (ink to white, white to ink) or a border thickening from 2px to 3px; danger is inverted ink, warning is a dashed ink frame, success a 3px ink frame.
- One motion moment: the homepage hero band clip-paths in left to right once, under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`.

## Colors

Two neutrals do almost all the work; two thread colours live inside the woven tiles, and one of them is allowed out as a line.

### Primary
- **Ink** (`{colors.ink}`): all type, all rules and borders (2px, 3px on state), button and chip fills, the announcement bar, badges and counts, the drawer scrim (`rgba(17,17,17,0.72)`), the lightbox ground (`rgba(17,17,17,0.94)`), the danger alert and danger status ground, the scrollbar thumb, the rotated diamond between brand names, the lead colour of the default band tile. Used at full strength, never tinted.
- **Cotton White** (`{colors.cotton-white}`): the page ground, the header and drawer ground, the tab bar ground, disabled and read-only input fill. Slightly warm so Paper frames and hover fills read as a lift against it.

### Secondary (the threads)
- **Thread Indigo** (`{colors.thread-indigo}`): dashes, chevrons and diamond chain in the default band; lead colour of the `--indigo` band tint. The one colour that leaves the weave, and only as a line: the 3px underline (emphasis `<mark>`, `.zh-thread`, links in prose and alerts, nav hover and `aria-current`, the current order-timeline step and current confirmation step, the sold-out "Notify me" 2px rule), the 6px bottom selvedge of the primary button and the toast, the phone tab bar's active 3px top rule, the text-selection ground, and the 3px `:focus-visible` outline. Never text, never a fill area, never a box border.
- **Thread Green** (`{colors.thread-green}`): diamond centres in the default band; lead colour of the `--green` band tint. Appears nowhere outside the band tiles.

### Neutral
- **Ink Soft** (`{colors.ink-soft}`): secondary text: ledes, ledger labels, fee labels, brand under a product name, counts, captions, meta lines, breadcrumb current item, step body copy, struck compare-at prices, placeholders, done step numerals, upcoming timeline labels.
- **Paper** (`{colors.paper}`): photo frame grounds, input and select fill, button text on ink, hover fill of the primary button, tag ground, alert and status ground, the sticky add-to-cart bar, the search suggestion list.

### Named Rules
**The Thread Stays in the Weave Rule.** Indigo and green exist as SVG fills inside the band tiles. Outside the tiles only indigo is allowed, and only as a line: the 3px underline, the 6px selvedge, the 3px active tab rule, the focus outline, the selection ground. Green never leaves the tiles. No thread colour is ever text, a background area, or a border around a box.

**The No Red, No Yellow Rule.** The storefront carries no red and no yellow in any form: not in tiles, not in alerts, not in error text, not in sale flags. Danger inverts to ink on white; sale and sold-out flags invert to ink; form errors are ink 600 with the indigo underline.

**The Two-Ink Rule.** Text is either Ink or Ink Soft. There is no third grey, no tinted text, no coloured link. Links are distinguished by weight (500 or 600) and the indigo underline, not by hue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Unbounded 600/700 (with Archivo, Helvetica Neue, Arial fallback), loaded site-wide by the shell
**Body Font:** Archivo 400/500/600/700 (with Helvetica Neue, Arial fallback)
**Amharic:** Noto Sans Ethiopic 400/600/700 replaces both stacks under `html[lang="am"]`; display headlines relax to line-height 1.15 and letter-spacing 0

**Character:** Unbounded is wide, heavy and tight-set, so headlines, prices and nav labels read as blocks stamped on cloth; Archivo underneath is plain and quiet. Unbounded carries every number that is a commitment (prices, fees, totals, order numbers, step numerals) and every command (buttons, nav, wordmark); Archivo carries everything a shopper reads.

### Hierarchy
- **Display Hero** (700, `clamp(27px, 7.3vw, 82px)` on phones, `clamp(44px, 5.6vw, 82px)` from 768px, line-height 1.0, -0.02em, `text-wrap: balance`, max 20ch): the homepage headline only. Its `<em>` is upright ink with the indigo underline.
- **Display** (700, `clamp(28px, 6vw, 56px)`, line-height 1.02, -0.02em, max 22ch): every other page `<h1>` (collection headings with the live count, cart, orders, account, FAQ, 404). The product title runs `clamp(26px, 5vw, 44px)`; the order number on the confirmation page runs `clamp(24px, 6.4vw, 56px)` tabular on its own line.
- **Headline** (700, `clamp(22px, 4vw, 34px)`, line-height 1.05, -0.02em): section titles. Directory and collection row names run `clamp(20px, 5vw, 36px)` at line-height 1 because each is a row, not a heading; the homepage channel heading `clamp(20px, 4vw, 30px)`; prose `<h2>` `clamp(20px, 3.2vw, 26px)`; the footer channel heading `clamp(20px, 3vw, 26px)`; summary, confirmation-block and empty-state headings 20px.
- **Title** (600, 14 to 19px, -0.01em, tabular numerals where numeric): fee ruler and tally values (17px), product price (15px; 19px on the lead module; 26px, 28px from 992px, on the product page; 18px in the sticky bar), ledger values (15px; 20px for the total), step titles, fieldset legends and status titles (16px), restock and review-form headings (18px), order numbers (16px), line totals (15px), brand names, FAQ summaries and account link rows (15px), address labels and product notes (14px). - **Meta** (400, 13px and 12.5px, +0.02em, Ink Soft): counts, toolbar summary, breadcrumbs, help text, bylines, order dates, legal line. Captions and fee labels 12px.
- **Label** (600, 12px, +0.06em, uppercase, Ink): every form label, table header (Ink Soft), footer language label, contact fact terms. Tags are 700, 10.5px, +0.06em uppercase (10px in the search suggestion type tag).
- **Nav** (Unbounded 600, 11px, +0.1em, uppercase): primary nav; the account link and cart button run +0.08em; drawer items 13px; footer column titles 11px; footer social links 11px +0.08em. The announcement bar is Archivo 600, 11px, +0.08em uppercase; the tab bar Archivo 600, 9.5px, +0.08em uppercase.
- **Button** (Unbounded 600, 12.5px, +0.02em, line-height 1.2): all `.zh-btn` labels; the small variant 11.5px.

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Truth Rule.** Every price, fee, total, count, size, quantity, order number and time carries `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums lining-nums` (`.zh-num`, or baked into the price, ruler, ledger and table-number styles). Money and commitments are set in Unbounded 600 so they align and read as facts, not footnotes.

**The Indigo Underline Rule.** Emphasis and links use one device: a 3px indigo underline, offset 0.14em (0.12em under row names), `text-decoration-skip-ink: none`. No bold-only emphasis, no coloured text, no italics. On hover the underline turns ink; on a hovered product module it appears under the title.

**The Loud Face Rule.** Unbounded is for what the shopper acts on or is promised: headings, money, nav, buttons, step numerals, order numbers. Reading copy, titles of products and lines, labels and meta stay in Archivo.

## Layout

Phone first at 390px; the desktop composition inherits from it. Content sits in `.zh-wrap` (max 1240px, 16px side padding; 24px from 768px). The woven bands run edge to edge; rules span the wrap.

- **Shell rhythm (every page):** ink announcement bar; header row (min-height 64px; 76px from 992px) as a three-column grid with the logo centred; Unbounded uppercase nav (hidden below 992px, when the drawer carries it); thin band (10px) closing the header, except on the homepage where the full hero band follows; page content; footer opening with the full band, then the channel row (hidden on the homepage, which has its own), a four-column ruled grid (2 columns on phones with the brand block spanning; `2fr 1fr 1fr 1fr` from 768px), and the legal line. Below 992px a fixed 62px tab bar sits on a 2px ink rule and the body carries matching bottom padding (plus 78px when the sticky add-to-cart bar is present).
- **Page head:** `padding: 26px 0 22px` (36/28 from 768px), 8px grid gap; `--ruled` variant closes with a 2px ink rule. Sections carry 40px top padding (56px from 1200px) and a heading row with `margin: 18px 0 20px`.
- **Grids:** `.zh-grid` is 2 columns on phones, 3 from 768px, 4 from 992px, gap `28px 16px` (20px column gap from 768px). Inside the catalog's filter layout the grid holds at 3 columns from 1024px beside a 260px filter column (40px gutter).
- **Rails:** on phones a horizontal snap rail bled to the viewport edge (`margin: 0 calc(50% - 50vw)`), columns `min(72vw, 300px)`, gap 16px, thin ink scrollbar; from 768px a 4-column dense grid with 28px row gap, and in the `--weave` variant the first module spans 2x2.
- **Rulers:** the fee ruler is 2 columns on phones, 4 from 768px; the orders tally 3 then 6; cells divided by 2px ink rules with 8px/12px padding; the `--stack` variant stays 2 columns.
- **Two-column pages (from 992px):** product page `7fr 5fr` with a 48px gutter and a sticky info column (`top: 24px`); cart and confirmation `7fr 5fr` with a 56px gutter (72px from 1200px) and a sticky summary; account `two` layout equal columns with 48/72px gutters from 768/1200px. Product details and reviews split `7fr 5fr` from 768px.
- **Steps:** stacked on phones (56px numeral column, 18px vertical padding); three ruled columns from 768px, except inside the product column where they stay stacked at 16px padding.
- **Filters:** a `<details>` block above the grid on phones whose toggle inverts when open; from 1024px `zent.js` opens it and hides the toggle so the groups read as a ruled left column (also after htmx swaps).
- **Narrow forms:** auth and checkout-adjacent forms cap at 440px; the review invite at 560px; prose at 68ch; FAQ at 76ch.
- **Band heights:** full band 56px on phones, 72px from 768px, 88px from 1200px; thin band 10px everywhere. Tiles repeat at the band's own height (`background-size: auto 100%`) so the weave is never stretched.
- **Spacing steps:** 4 / 6 / 10 / 16 / 20 / 28 px inside components; 40 / 56 px between sections; 18px form field gap; 12 to 16px row padding.

## Elevation & Depth

There are no shadows anywhere on the storefront. Depth is physical: the homepage's three hero swatches overlap by 22px, step down by 14/26px (28/56px from 768px) and are stacked by z-index; under each one, and under the product page's main photo, a thin woven tile is offset 12px down and right so the photo reads as cloth laid on a strip of cloth. Everywhere else, edges are drawn: 2px ink borders around photos, inputs, chips and buttons; 2px ink rules between rows, ruler cells, list items, table rows and steps. Overlays are ink at reduced opacity (drawer scrim 0.72, lightbox 0.94), never blurred. Fixed surfaces (sticky add-to-cart bar, tab bar, sticky summary column) separate themselves from the page with a 2px ink rule, not a shadow. State is shown by inversion or by a border thickening from 2px to 3px, never by a lift.

### Named Rules
**The Overlap Not Shadow Rule.** If two things need to read as layered, one overlaps the other and both keep a 2px ink edge. `box-shadow`, `filter: blur` and gradients do not appear in this system, at rest or on hover.

**The Ink Scrim Rule.** Anything that dims the page (drawer, lightbox) is ink at 0.72 or 0.94 opacity; no colour, no blur, no radius on what sits above it.

## Shapes

Square. Every corner on the storefront is 0px: buttons, inputs, selects, chips, tags, badges, photo frames, alerts, the toast, the drawer, the sticky bar, the empty-state box. The single radius in the stylesheets is the 2px on the `:focus-visible` outline. Borders are 2px solid ink; 3px on state (invalid input, hovered or active thumbnail, hovered swatch, current timeline mark, success alert); 2px dashed for the empty state and the warning alert. Marks are squares: the 14px timeline square (filled when done, 3px-bordered when current), the 8px rotated ink square between brand names, the 12px square spinner, the FAQ's rotating plus. Photos are 4:5 portraits, cover-fitted, on a Paper frame; thumbnails and swatches are 56, 72, 88 or 96px squares. The band supplies the only ornament: chevrons, dashes, a diamond chain and selvedge lines, in ink, indigo and green.

## Components

### Buttons
Ink bars set in the display face; the primary is hemmed with indigo.
- **Shape:** square (0px), 2px ink border, min-height 50px, `inline-flex` with a 10px gap for an optional leading icon (15px).
- **Primary (`.zh-btn--primary`):** ink fill, Paper text, `padding: 0 18px` (0 22px from 768px), plus a **6px indigo bottom border, the selvedge**. Inside a danger status it inverts to Paper on ink with the selvedge kept.
- **Ghost (`.zh-btn--ghost`):** transparent fill, ink text, same border and size.
- **Small (`.zh-btn--sm`):** min-height 40px, `padding: 0 14px` (0 16px from 768px), 11.5px label.
- **Block (`.zh-btn--block`):** full width; used for Add to cart and Place order.
- **Icon (`.zh-iconbtn`):** 44px square, 2px ink border, transparent fill, 16px glyph; `--flat` drops the border (the header search submit). The product module's save button is the same idea at 36px on a Paper ground.
- **Hover / Focus:** inversion. Primary becomes Paper with ink text (the selvedge stays indigo); ghost and icon buttons become ink with Paper text. `:active` translates down 1px. Transitions: transform 220ms on the world ease `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, colour 160ms (140ms on icon buttons and chips). Disabled drops to 0.45 opacity; an in-flight htmx submit to 0.6.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** build every storefront page on `body.zh-body` with the `.zh-` prefix: shell and components from `zent-tibeb.css`, page layout in `zent-tibeb-pages.css`; keep the Control Room on its own stylesheets.
- **Do** close the header with the thin band and open the footer with the full band on every page; vary a section with the `--indigo` or `--green` tint, never with a new colour.
- **Do** set every number a shopper acts on (price, fee, total, count, size, quantity, order number, days, hours) in tabular lining figures, and set money and commitments in Unbounded 600.
- **Do** emphasise and link with the 3px indigo underline; mark the primary action with the 6px indigo selvedge; mark the current nav item, tab and timeline step with indigo as a line.
- **Do** show state by inverting ink and Paper or by thickening a 2px border to 3px; show danger as inverted ink, warning as a dashed ink frame, success as a 3px ink frame.
- **Do** separate rows with 2px ink rules and frame photos, inputs, chips and buttons with 2px ink borders; layer by overlap.
- **Do** keep the radio-chip pattern accessible: the input stays in the DOM, visually hidden, and drives `label.zh-chip` through `:checked` and `:focus-visible`.
- **Do** keep the empty state honest: the dashed ink box with one sentence, never placeholder products, testimonials or metrics.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use `box-shadow`, gradients, blur or any border-radius; every corner is 0px and the only radius is the 2px focus outline.
- **Don't** use red or yellow anywhere on the storefront: not for errors, sale flags, alerts, badges or tiles.
- **Don't** use indigo as text, a fill area or a box border; it is a line (3px underline, 6px selvedge, 3px tab rule, focus outline) or the selection ground. Don't use green outside the band tiles at all.
- **Don't** introduce a third text colour; text is Ink or Ink Soft, and links differ by weight and underline, not hue.
- **Don't** box content into cards or boxed sidebars; the filter column, the summary and the account panels are ruled, not framed.
- **Don't** set reading copy, product titles or labels in Unbounded, and don't set headings, money or buttons in Archivo.
- **Don't** describe the fixture's placeholder photographs or the legacy `asset/images/banner.webp` as the brand's imagery; the band and the products carry every viewport.
